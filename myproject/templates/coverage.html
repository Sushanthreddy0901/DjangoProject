Good questions

Before you decide.

A few useful things to know while you compare health plans.

How do I know which plan is right for me?

Think about the care you use, your preferred doctors and facilities, prescriptions, and what you can budget monthly and when you need care. Compare those needs against the official plan documents.

Does a lower monthly premium always mean a lower-cost plan?

Not necessarily. Deductibles, copayments, coinsurance, network rules, and services that are not covered can also affect what you pay. Review the full cost-sharing details, not only the premium.

How can I check whether my doctor is in network?

Use the insurer's current provider directory and confirm directly with both the provider and insurer before receiving care. Network information can change.

Where can I find the official coverage details?

Review the plan's Summary of Benefits and Coverage, policy or contract, drug list, and provider directory. Those documents explain the coverage, exclusions, and cost sharing for that specific plan.
