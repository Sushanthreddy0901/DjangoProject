Support for real life
Why it matters

Health needs can be hard to predict.

Health insurance is an agreement that helps pay for eligible medical care. You pay a regular premium, and your plan shares the cost of covered services according to its rules. Having coverage can make care and its costs easier to plan for, especially when an unexpected need comes up.

The benefits

More support when it counts.

Plans differ, but health coverage can offer several kinds of support.

01 / PLAN AHEAD

Manage the cost of care

Share eligible costs with your plan instead of facing every covered medical bill on your own. Premiums and out-of-pocket costs depend on the plan.

02 / STAY WELL

Support preventive care

Many plans include preventive services, such as screenings and checkups. What is covered and what you pay can depend on your plan and where you receive care.

03 / FIND CARE

Know where to turn

A provider network can help you find doctors, clinics, and hospitals connected to your plan, making it easier to understand your options.

Choose with confidence

A plan should fit your life, not just your budget.

Before choosing, consider the care you use, your prescriptions, the doctors you prefer, and what you can afford each month and when you need care.

Compare the premium, deductible, copayments, out-of-pocket maximum, and provider network. These details show more of the cost and access picture than the monthly premium alone.

Coverage, costs, eligibility, and provider networks vary by plan and location. This page is general information, not a quote or a guarantee of benefits. Review the official plan documents before enrolling.
